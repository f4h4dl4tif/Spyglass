Spyglass is installed

Spyglass is now open. Press ⌥Space in any app for Quick Search. Its icon (a folder with a spyglass) sits in the menu bar with your recent downloads.

Optional: to let Space Map and Clean Up see every folder, give Spyglass Full Disk Access in System Settings › Privacy & Security.

To start Spyglass automatically, open Spyglass › Settings › General and turn on Open Spyglass at login.
