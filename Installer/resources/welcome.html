Spyglass

Spyglass is a file browser for your Mac, built to sit alongside Finder. It has tabs, icon, list and column views, Quick Look, an Inspector and Finder tags. On top of that: a Home dashboard of your library, plain-English search (⌥Space from anywhere), Downloaded From, Photos, searchable text in screenshots, split view with a transfer queue, Clean Up with undo, Duplicates and Blurry Photos, Space Map, an App Uninstaller, Mac Health, Routines, an Assistant powered by Apple Intelligence, Shortcuts and Siri actions, and 69 on-device tools — including Summarize, Ask About a Document, Sign and Redact PDF, Record Screen, Transcribe and Smart Rename.

Nothing is uploaded. Everything it removes goes to the Trash and can be put back.

This installer puts Spyglass in your Applications folder and opens it when it's done.

Requires macOS 14 Sonoma or later. Runs on Apple Silicon and Intel Macs.
